Simple & Automated

How PitchVision Works

In four straightforward steps, regular broadcast video is transformed into clean, actionable football intelligence.

01

Detect Players & Ball

Computer vision detects all 22 players, goalkeepers, referees, and the match ball in under 50ms, identifying their precise foot positions on the grass.

02

Separate the Teams

The system analyzes jersey colors, filters out the green grass background, and automatically groups players into Team A and Team B without any manual tags.

03

2D Pitch Projection

Geometric homography removes camera angle distortion and maps player positions onto a standardized 105m × 68m top-down tactical pitch.

04

Tactical Insights

Calculates key metrics including team width, compactness, defensive line height, and tactical shape (like 4-3-3 or 4-2-3-1) in real time.
