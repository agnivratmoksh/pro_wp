Core Objectives

What We Are Building

PitchVision breaks down the complex challenge of computerized football analytics into five cohesive, mathematically grounded objectives.

OBJECTIVE 01

Football Object Detection

Deep learning localization capable of identifying all relevant entities in broadcast match frames with high precision and sub-pixel ground contact localization.

  • Outfield players, goalkeepers & referees
  • High-speed small-object ball localization
  • YOLOv8 transfer learning on match datasets
OBJECTIVE 02

Unsupervised Team Classification

Automatic separation of players into their respective teams without manual labeling using color histogram extraction and unsupervised clustering.

  • Torso crop HSV color space extraction
  • Pitch turf green hue masking & filtering
  • K-Means dominant color clustering
OBJECTIVE 03

Pitch Understanding & Homography

Projective geometric transformation mapping skewed perspective camera pixels onto a standardized 2D top-down (105m × 68m) tactical field representation.

  • Field line and keypoint landmark detection
  • 3×3 Planar Homography matrix estimation
  • Metric real-world coordinate normalization
OBJECTIVE 04

Formation Detection

Inferring team tactical structures (e.g. 4-3-3, 4-4-2, 4-2-3-1, 3-5-2) using spatial player clustering, line regression, and positional topology matching.

  • Defensive, midfield & forward line grouping
  • Dynamic in-possession shape estimation
  • Visual & textual tactical descriptions
OBJECTIVE 05

Tactical Insights & Analytics

Automated intelligence generation from 2D coordinates: computing team width, convex hull compactness, defensive line height, and midfield dominance.

  • Team width (m) & length stretch metrics
  • Convex Hull compactness surface area (m²)
  • Zone 14 & midfield overload indicators

Stage 1: YOLO Object Detection

Ultralytics YOLOv8 PyTorch Transfer Learning

PitchVision deploys a customized YOLO deep neural network fine-tuned on professional football datasets to detect and segment players, referees, goalkeepers, and the football in sub-50ms latency.

Detection Targets

Localized entities include outfield players, goalkeepers (special kit), referees (fluorescent yellow/black), and the match ball with sub-pixel precision.

Output Features

Produces bounding coordinates [x_min, y_min, x_max, y_max], class logits, object confidence scores, and player foot-contact ground points.

Evolution & Horizons

Future Scope & Expansion Roadmap

PitchVision establishes a powerful computer vision foundation that naturally evolves into a comprehensive full-match football analytics suite.

PHASE 01

Multi-Object Video Tracking

Integration of ByteTrack and BoT-SORT for persistent player identity tracking through occlusions and fast transitions across continuous 90-minute broadcast video.

PHASE 02

3D Ball Physics & Trajectory

Polynomial trajectory regression and 3D flight modeling to infer aerial passes, shot velocity (km/h), and curl physics directly from 2D camera perspectives.

PHASE 03

Pass Networks & Pitch Control

Graph Neural Network (GNN) pass probability modeling combined with Voronoi pitch dominance tessellations to calculate open passing lanes and pressing intensity.

PHASE 04

xG & Automated Scouting

Spatial Expected Goals (xG) algorithms and generative tactical scouting reports delivering comprehensive pre-match and post-match opposition debriefs.
